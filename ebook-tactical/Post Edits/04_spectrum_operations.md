    19|The Reactive Human operates on a logic of elimination. When they feel a "negative" state—such as anger, fear, or grief—their instinct is to destroy it. They try to suppress the feeling, distract themselves from it, or "kill" the emotion.

    23|The Sovereign Operator does not eliminate; they **transmute**.
